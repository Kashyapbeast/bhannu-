No More Avoiding It

I'm sorry.

Not the quick kind of sorry we throw around to end an argument — an actual one. I'm sorry for the moments I was short with you, for not listening properly, and for letting distance sit between us instead of closing it. You deserved better from me, and I want to be better, starting now.
